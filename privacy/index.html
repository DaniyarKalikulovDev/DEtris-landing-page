Privacy Policy — DEtris

Last updated: 27 August 2026

DEtris does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What is stored

The app saves a small amount of information on your device only:

  • your settings (music, sound, vibration, ghost piece, touch sensitivity, tap zones, selected music track)
  • your highest score
  • whether you have completed the tutorial
  • the playback position of the selected music track

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

No accounts, no tracking

DEtris has no sign-up and no login. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no software development kits that collect data.

No network access

DEtris works fully offline. The app makes no network requests.

Permissions

DEtris requests no device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or files.

Children

DEtris is safe for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
